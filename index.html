Ask Wander Wave
Where is the Wander Wave Tours office in Cairo?

Wander Wave Tours is listed on Google Maps at 2FX7+HPV, El Sheikh Abdel Gelil Eissa Street, New Cairo 1, Cairo Governorate 11835, Egypt. Use the Get Directions button on this page for the current map pin.

How can I contact Wander Wave to plan a trip?

Send your destination, travel dates and number of travelers through the trip request form or contact the team on WhatsApp at +20 10 70752150.

What travel services does Wander Wave offer?

Wander Wave helps with custom travel packages, luxury trips, honeymoon planning, family vacations, group travel, hotel and resort bookings, and travel guidance.

Can Wander Wave plan a honeymoon or a custom itinerary?

Yes. You can request an itinerary tailored to your destination, dates, travelers, preferred accommodation and budget.

Does Wander Wave work with travelers outside Cairo?

Wander Wave describes its travel planning services as operating from Cairo and Abu Dhabi. Contact the team for assistance with your specific route or destination.

How can I arrange an office visit?

Contact Wander Wave on WhatsApp to arrange an in-person consultation and confirm available appointment times before visiting.
